📸 STICKER STOP — Product Images
===================================

Place your sticker sheet images in this folder.
The expected filenames are:

  space-sheet-a.jpg          ← Space Exploration, Sheet A (NASA classics)
  space-sheet-b.jpg          ← Space Exploration, Sheet B (modern space)

  cookie-mouse-sheet-a.jpg   ← If You Give a Mouse a Cookie, Sheet A
  cookie-mouse-sheet-b.jpg   ← If You Give a Mouse a Cookie, Sheet B

  cute-animals-sheet-a.jpg   ← Cute Animals & Flowers, Sheet A (forest)
  cute-animals-sheet-b.jpg   ← Cute Animals & Flowers, Sheet B (beach)

  alien-adventure-sheet-a.jpg  ← Alien Adventure, Sheet A
  alien-adventure-sheet-b.jpg  ← Alien Adventure, Sheet B

  kingdom-quest-sheet-a.jpg  ← Kingdom Quest, Sheet A (heroes)
  kingdom-quest-sheet-b.jpg  ← Kingdom Quest, Sheet B (villains)

Tips:
  - JPG, PNG, or WebP all work — just make sure the filename matches exactly.
  - Recommended size: around 800×1100px (portrait orientation).
  - If an image is missing the site still works — it just shows a placeholder emoji.
